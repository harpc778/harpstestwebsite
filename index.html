How I Used AI to Pass My Canadian Citizenship Test With 100%

Harpreet Singh Chatha · 2026 ·
AI Canada Citizenship

Growing up in England and walking the streets of Leicester, I never really thought I'd move to Canada.

Six and a half years after I made the trip from LHR to YVR, I'm officially a Canadian.

On my journey to becoming a Canadian, I had to take a Citizenship test. Enter AI.

I used AI to help me practice for the test, and in the end, I passed with 100%.

I'm not one for bragging, but that's probably a much better score than some people who use a hashtag that rhymes with #trucktrudeau on Social Media would get.

How I Actually Used AI to Study

I fed the Discover Canada textbook to Claude and ChatGPT and then asked the AI models to make me study cards, questions, and answers.

I'm a visual learner, so I asked the AI to code practice quizzes into HTML format. I then pasted the code into a tool called Code Beautify to practice interactively until I felt ready for the test.

💡 If you think that sounds like too much work — did you know you can also turn the Discover Canada textbook into a podcast?

Yeah, that's right. A podcast, thanks to NotebookLM. So if you're a learner who does better by listening, you can upload the Discover Canada textbook into NotebookLM and they'll make a podcast for you. You can then download and listen to it whenever you're free.

Take the Practice Test

If you found this page because you're looking to take a mock or practice test, then you're in luck. I've added a practice test below that was created with the help of AI.

If you landed on this page by chance, then why not take the test anyway to see how much you know about Canada? It includes questions similar to those found in the real thing.
