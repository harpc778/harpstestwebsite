llms.txt Generator

Fill in your site details, sections and key links, or import them from a URL list or sitemap, and get a valid llms.txt file you can copy or download.

⚠️ Heads up: an llms.txt file does not help you show up in AI search. No major AI platform has confirmed it uses llms.txt for retrieval or citations, and there is no evidence it improves visibility. Put your effort into crawlable, well structured content, strong entities and schema instead. But if you must make one (a client or stakeholder asked, or you just want to experiment), this generator will build a valid file for you.
