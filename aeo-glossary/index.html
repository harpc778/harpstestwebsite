70 AI search and SEO terms, explained in plain English.

A

Agentic commerce

AI Search & Models

Shopping in which an AI agent carries out tasks on a user’s behalf, such as finding products, comparing options and, with authorization, completing purchases.

Related: Agentic search · AI search · Brand recommendation

Agentic search

AI Search & Models

A search experience in which an AI system carries out multiple research steps on a user’s behalf. It can search different sources, compare findings and refine its approach to complete a task.

Related: Query fan-out · Agentic commerce · AI search

Learn more: Something Strange Is Happening in Search Console →

AI citation

Measurement

A reference to a source displayed in an AI-generated answer, often as a clickable link. A citation identifies a source but does not guarantee that the answer accurately represents it.

Related: Citation rate · Citation share · Brand mention

AI crawler

Crawlers & Technical

An automated bot that accesses web content for an AI-related purpose, such as search discovery or collecting potential training data. Different crawlers have different purposes and access controls.

Related: GPTBot · OAI-SearchBot · ClaudeBot · robots.txt

Learn more: Robots.txt Generator →

AI hallucination

AI Search & Models

Incorrect or unsupported information generated by an AI system and presented as though it were factual. Hallucinations can include invented statistics, sources, products or events.

Related: Grounding · Parametric knowledge · Large language model (LLM)

AI referral traffic

Measurement

Visits to your website that originate from links in AI platforms such as ChatGPT or Perplexity. Analytics tools can identify some of these visits, although attribution may be incomplete.

Related: AI visibility · Zero-click search · Branded search demand

AI search

AI Search & Models

A search experience that uses artificial intelligence to find, interpret and present information. It can combine generated answers with links, product recommendations and opportunities to ask follow-up questions.

Related: Answer engine · Google AI Overviews · Query fan-out · ChatGPT search

AI SEO

Strategy & Concepts

A term used for both applying AI tools to SEO tasks and optimizing a website’s visibility in AI search experiences. The intended meaning depends on the context.

Related: Answer engine optimization (AEO) · Generative engine optimization (GEO) · Search engine optimization (SEO)

Learn more: Good SEO is Good GEO →

AI share of voice

Measurement

A measure of your brand’s presence relative to competitors across a defined set of AI prompts and responses. Calculations vary between tools, so the prompts, platforms and measurement method should be specified.

Related: AI visibility · Prompt tracking · Citation share

Learn more: AEO Leaderboard →

AI visibility

Measurement

The extent to which a brand, website or piece of content appears in AI-generated responses. It can include mentions, citations and recommendations across the platforms and prompts being monitored.

Related: AI citation · Brand mention · Brand recommendation · AI share of voice

Learn more: AI Visibility Measurement Planner prompt →

Answer engine

AI Search & Models

A system that responds directly to a question rather than only displaying a list of links. Answer engines can use search results, structured information and AI models to produce their responses.

Related: Answer engine optimization (AEO) · AI search · Featured snippet

Answer engine optimization (AEO)

Strategy & Concepts

The practice of improving content and a brand’s online presence to increase their chances of appearing in answers provided by search engines, AI assistants and other answer-based systems.

Related: Generative engine optimization (GEO) · Search engine optimization (SEO) · Answer engine

Learn more: Good SEO is Good GEO →

Applebot-Extended

Crawlers & Technical

A robots.txt control token that lets website owners manage whether content collected by Applebot can be used to train Apple’s generative AI models. It does not control Applebot’s crawling or inclusion in Apple’s search results.

Related: Google-Extended · robots.txt · Training data

Learn more: Robots.txt Generator →

B

Bingbot

Crawlers & Technical

Microsoft’s web crawler for discovering and updating pages in Bing’s search index. Making content accessible to Bingbot supports its discovery in Bing-powered search experiences.

Related: Crawlability · Indexability · IndexNow

Brand mention

Content & Brand

A reference to a brand within content or an AI-generated response. A mention can appear without a link, citation or recommendation.

Related: AI citation · Brand recommendation · Digital PR

Brand recommendation

Content & Brand

An answer that suggests a brand, product or service as a suitable choice. A recommendation goes beyond naming a brand by presenting it as an option for the user’s needs.

Related: Brand mention · Brand sentiment · AI visibility

Brand sentiment

Content & Brand

The tone and opinion expressed about a brand, such as positive, negative, neutral or mixed. In AI search, sentiment describes how the answer portrays the brand.

Related: Brand recommendation · Third-party reviews · User-generated content (UGC)

Branded search demand

Measurement

The volume of searches that include a brand’s name or recognizable variations of it. Changes can indicate shifts in awareness or interest, but do not establish which marketing activity caused them.

Related: Brand mention · AI referral traffic · Digital PR

C

ChatGPT search

AI Search & Models

ChatGPT’s capability to search the web and use retrieved information to generate responses with source links. It differs from responses generated without searching the web.

Related: OAI-SearchBot · AI search · Grounding

ChatGPT-User

Crawlers & Technical

A user agent OpenAI uses for certain user-triggered actions, including accessing web pages. It is separate from OpenAI’s automated search and training crawlers.

Related: OAI-SearchBot · GPTBot · AI crawler

Chunking

AI Search & Models

Dividing content into smaller passages so a retrieval system can process, store and retrieve relevant sections. The way content is divided can affect whether a passage retains enough context to be useful.

Related: Embeddings · Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) · Context window

Citation rate

Measurement

The percentage of measured AI responses that cite a particular website or page. For this glossary, it means responses containing at least one citation to the site divided by all responses tested.

Related: Citation share · AI citation · Prompt tracking

Learn more: AI Visibility Measurement Planner prompt →

Citation share

Measurement

The percentage of citations attributed to a website out of all citations in a defined set of AI responses or grounding queries. It measures a site’s share of citations rather than how many answers mention its brand.

Related: Citation rate · AI citation · AI share of voice

Learn more: AI Visibility Measurement Planner prompt →

ClaudeBot

Crawlers & Technical

Anthropic’s automated crawler for collecting web content that may contribute to model training. It is separate from Claude-SearchBot, which supports search, and Claude-User, which accesses content for user-triggered requests.

Related: AI crawler · GPTBot · Training data

Learn more: Robots.txt Generator →

Comparison content

Content & Brand

Content that explains the differences between products, services, brands or approaches. Useful comparisons evaluate relevant factors such as features, pricing, limitations and suitability for different needs.

Related: Search intent · Third-party reviews · Brand recommendation

Learn more: Commercial Page Optimizer prompt →

Context window

AI Search & Models

The maximum amount of information an AI model can process in a single interaction, measured in tokens. Depending on the system, this can include instructions, conversation history, retrieved content and the generated response.

Related: Chunking · Prompt · Large language model (LLM)

Crawlability

Crawlers & Technical

How easily automated crawlers can access a website’s pages and resources. Crawlability can be affected by links, robots.txt instructions, server errors, authentication and security controls.

Related: Indexability · robots.txt · JavaScript rendering

Learn more: How To Perform An SEO Audit For Free →

D

Digital PR

Content & Brand

The practice of earning online coverage, links and brand mentions through newsworthy stories, research, expert commentary or useful resources. In SEO, digital PR often aims to earn relevant editorial backlinks.

Related: Brand mention · Information gain · Third-party reviews

Learn more: Digital PR Opportunity Finder prompt →

E

E-E-A-T

Strategy & Concepts

Experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trustworthiness. These are qualities Google’s search quality raters consider when evaluating content. E-E-A-T is not a single ranking factor or a publicly available website score.

Related: Topical authority · Information gain · Third-party reviews

Embeddings

AI Search & Models

Numerical representations of text, images or other content that encode patterns and relationships. Retrieval systems can use embeddings to find related content even when its wording differs from the query.

Related: Chunking · Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) · Entity

Entity

Strategy & Concepts

A distinct person, organization, place, product or concept that a system can identify. Entities help systems distinguish between things with similar names and understand their relationships.

Related: Knowledge graph · Structured data / schema markup · Embeddings

Learn more: Improving Salience Scores →

F

Featured snippet

AI Search & Models

A highlighted excerpt from a web page that Google displays in some search results to answer a question directly. It is distinct from an AI Overview, which generates a response rather than simply displaying an excerpt.

Related: Google AI Overviews · Zero-click search · Answer engine

G

Generative AI

AI Search & Models

Artificial intelligence that generates content such as text, images, audio or code based on patterns learned during training and information supplied during use.

Related: Large language model (LLM) · AI hallucination · Training data

Generative engine optimization (GEO)

Strategy & Concepts

The practice of improving content and a brand’s online presence to increase visibility in responses generated by AI systems. It overlaps substantially with AEO and SEO.

Related: Answer engine optimization (AEO) · Large language model optimization (LLMO) · Search engine optimization (SEO)

Learn more: Good SEO is Good GEO →

Google AI Mode

AI Search & Models

A conversational search experience within Google Search that generates responses to questions and supports further exploration through follow-up questions and links to websites.

Related: Google AI Overviews · Query fan-out · AI search

Learn more: GEO, AEO & AI SEO: What's Real, What's Hype →

Google AI Overviews

AI Search & Models

AI-generated summaries that appear for some Google searches, alongside links to supporting sources. They aim to give users an overview of a topic or question.

Related: Google AI Mode · Featured snippet · Zero-click search

Learn more: GEO, AEO & AI SEO: What's Real, What's Hype →

Google-Extended

Crawlers & Technical

A robots.txt control token for managing whether content Google crawls can be used for certain Gemini training and grounding purposes. It is not a separate crawler and does not control inclusion in Google Search, AI Overviews or AI Mode.

Related: Applebot-Extended · robots.txt · Grounding

Learn more: Robots.txt Generator →

GPTBot

Crawlers & Technical

OpenAI’s automated crawler for collecting web content that may be used to train its generative AI models. Its robots.txt setting is independent of OAI-SearchBot’s search access setting.

Related: OAI-SearchBot · ChatGPT-User · robots.txt

Learn more: Robots.txt Generator →

Grounding

AI Search & Models

Connecting an AI response to external information, such as retrieved web pages, documents or databases. Grounding can improve accuracy and relevance but does not eliminate errors.

Related: Grounding query · Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) · AI hallucination

Grounding query

AI Search & Models

A search query an AI system uses to retrieve information that supports its response. It may differ from the question originally entered by the user.

Related: Query fan-out · Grounding · Citation share

I

Indexability

Crawlers & Technical

Whether a page is eligible to be included in a search engine’s index. Being indexable does not guarantee that a search engine will index the page or display it in results.

Related: Crawlability · Sitemap (XML) · IndexNow

Learn more: How To Perform An SEO Audit For Free →

IndexNow

Crawlers & Technical

An open protocol that lets websites notify participating search engines when URLs are added, updated or deleted. It helps communicate changes but does not guarantee crawling or indexing.

Related: Bingbot · Sitemap (XML) · Indexability

Information gain

Strategy & Concepts

The additional useful information content provides beyond what is already available. In content planning, this can come from original research, firsthand experience, new examples or a more complete explanation.

Related: E-E-A-T · Topical authority · Digital PR

Learn more: AI SEO at Scale: the Wix case study →

Internal linking

Crawlers & Technical

Linking from one page to another on the same website. Internal links help users and search engines discover content and understand how pages relate to each other.

Related: Crawlability · Topical authority · Sitemap (XML)

Learn more: How To Perform An SEO Audit For Free →

J

JavaScript rendering

Crawlers & Technical

Running a page’s JavaScript to produce its content and functionality. Crawlers differ in their ability to render JavaScript, which can affect whether they can access content that depends on it.

Related: Crawlability · AI crawler · Indexability

Learn more: Vibe Code SEO Checker prompt →

K

Knowledge graph

Strategy & Concepts

A structured collection of entities and the relationships between them. A knowledge graph can connect a company to its products, founders, locations and other associated information.

Related: Entity · Structured data / schema markup · Brand mention

Learn more: Improving Salience Scores →

L

Large language model (LLM)

AI Search & Models

An AI model trained on large amounts of data to process and generate language. It produces responses using learned patterns and supplied context, and can generate plausible information that is incorrect.

Related: Parametric knowledge · Training data · Context window

Large language model optimization (LLMO)

Strategy & Concepts

An industry term for efforts to improve how brands or content appear in responses from large language models. Its meaning varies and often overlaps with AEO and GEO.

Related: Generative engine optimization (GEO) · Answer engine optimization (AEO) · AI SEO

Learn more: Good SEO is Good GEO →

llms.txt

Crawlers & Technical

A proposed Markdown file convention for giving AI systems concise website information and links to useful resources. It is not an access-control file or a requirement for appearing in Google’s AI search features.

Related: robots.txt · AI crawler · Sitemap (XML)

Learn more: llms.txt Generator →

M

Model Context Protocol (MCP)

AI Search & Models

An open standard for connecting AI applications to external tools and data sources. It enables compatible applications to retrieve information and perform actions through connected services.

Related: Agentic search · Large language model (LLM) · Prompt

Learn more: SEO Intelligence Starter Kit →

O

OAI-SearchBot

Crawlers & Technical

OpenAI’s automated crawler used to help surface websites in ChatGPT’s search features. Allowing it supports search discovery but does not guarantee inclusion or citation.

Related: GPTBot · ChatGPT search · ChatGPT-User

Learn more: Robots.txt Generator →

P

Parametric knowledge

AI Search & Models

Information encoded in a model’s parameters through training, rather than retrieved from an external source when answering a question. It can be incomplete or outdated and is not a searchable database of training documents.

Related: Training data · Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) · AI hallucination

PerplexityBot

Crawlers & Technical

Perplexity’s automated crawler used to discover content and surface websites in its search results. It is separate from Perplexity-User, which handles user-triggered access, and is not used to collect training data for foundation models.

Related: AI crawler · OAI-SearchBot · robots.txt

Learn more: Robots.txt Generator →

Personalization

AI Search & Models

Adjusting results or responses using information about a user, such as their location, preferences, conversation history or connected data. The information used depends on the platform, permissions and settings.

Related: Prompt tracking · AI search · Context window

Prompt

AI Search & Models

The input supplied to an AI system, such as a question, instruction or request. A prompt can also include examples, source material and other context to guide the response.

Related: Prompt tracking · Prompt injection · Context window

Learn more: AI Prompt Library →

Prompt injection

AI Search & Models

An attempt to redirect an AI system’s behaviour through instructions embedded in content it encounters, such as a web page, document or email. These instructions may conflict with the user’s original request.

Related: Prompt · Agentic search · AI crawler

Prompt tracking

Measurement

Repeatedly testing a defined set of prompts to monitor how brands, websites or competitors appear in AI responses. Results represent the tested sample and can change between runs.

Related: AI share of voice · Citation rate · AI visibility

Learn more: AI Visibility Measurement Planner prompt →

Q

Query fan-out

AI Search & Models

A technique in which an AI search system issues multiple related searches across subtopics or sources to help answer one question. These searches can retrieve information beyond the user’s original wording.

Related: Grounding query · AI search · Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)

Learn more: What Is Query Fan Out & Why It Matters →

R

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)

AI Search & Models

A method that retrieves relevant information from external sources and supplies it to an AI model as context for generating a response. It allows the system to use information beyond what it learned during training.

Related: Grounding · Embeddings · Chunking

robots.txt

Crawlers & Technical

A website file that tells compliant crawlers which paths they may access. It controls crawling rather than directly controlling indexing, and it does not secure private information.

Related: AI crawler · Crawlability · Google-Extended

Learn more: Robots.txt Generator →

S

Scaled content abuse

Strategy & Concepts

Creating many pages primarily to manipulate search rankings rather than help users. Google’s policy applies regardless of whether the content is produced by AI, humans or a combination of both.

Related: Information gain · Topical authority · E-E-A-T

Learn more: AI SEO at Scale: the Wix case study →

Search engine optimization (SEO)

Strategy & Concepts

The practice of improving a website’s accessibility, content and online presence to increase its visibility in organic search results and attract relevant visitors.

Related: Answer engine optimization (AEO) · Generative engine optimization (GEO) · Search intent

Search intent

Strategy & Concepts

The goal behind a search or question, such as finding information, comparing options, locating a website or making a purchase. Content should address what the user is trying to accomplish.

Related: Comparison content · Query fan-out · Zero-click search

Sitemap (XML)

Crawlers & Technical

A machine-readable file listing website URLs, sometimes with information such as their last modification dates. It helps search engines discover content but does not guarantee crawling or indexing.

Related: IndexNow · Indexability · robots.txt

Learn more: How To Perform An SEO Audit For Free →

Structured data / schema markup

Crawlers & Technical

Machine-readable information added to a page to describe its content, such as a product, organization or article. Schema.org provides a widely used vocabulary for this markup.

Related: Entity · Knowledge graph · Featured snippet

Learn more: Schema Markup Generator →

T

Third-party reviews

Content & Brand

Evaluations of a product, service or business published by someone other than the company itself. Their independence, evidence and commercial relationships should be considered when assessing credibility.

Related: User-generated content (UGC) · Brand sentiment · Comparison content

Learn more: AI Gift Guide Writer prompt →

Topical authority

Strategy & Concepts

An industry term describing a website’s perceived expertise, credibility and depth of coverage on a subject. It is not a publicly available Google score, and publishing more pages alone does not establish it.

Related: E-E-A-T · Information gain · Internal linking

Training data

AI Search & Models

Information used to teach an AI model patterns during training. It is distinct from information retrieved through web search, uploaded documents or connected tools when the model answers a question.

Related: Parametric knowledge · GPTBot · Large language model (LLM)

U

User-generated content (UGC)

Content & Brand

Content created by users or customers, such as reviews, forum discussions, comments and social posts. It can provide firsthand perspectives, although its accuracy and authenticity vary.

Related: Third-party reviews · Brand sentiment · Brand mention

Z

Zero-click search

Measurement

A search that ends without the user clicking through to an external website. This can happen when the results page answers the question directly or the user stops searching.

Related: Google AI Overviews · Featured snippet · AI referral traffic
