Digital PR Opportunity Finder

Find five data-led story ideas that journalists and industry writers are likely to cite and link to.

  • Works with: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, with web browsing on
  • You’ll need: your business type and target market or location
Prompt
Develop five digital PR ideas for a [business type] serving [market/location]. Focus on useful statistics pages, original comparisons or analyses of public data that journalists and industry writers could reference. For each idea, provide: the angle, who would cite it and why, available data sources, proposed methodology and a useful chart or table. Browse to confirm the suggested datasets exist and are accessible. Separate ideas we can produce with public data from those requiring a survey or proprietary data. Recommend the strongest idea based on relevance, feasibility and potential to earn editorial links.

How to use it

  1. Tap Copy prompt.
  2. Paste it into your AI tool and replace everything in [brackets] with your details.
  3. Attach any files the prompt mentions, then run it.
