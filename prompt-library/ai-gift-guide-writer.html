AI Gift Guide Writer

Turn product pages and real customer feedback into an accurate, SEO-friendly gift guide, with research and an outline before any writing.

  • Works with: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini (any tool that can read uploaded files)
  • You’ll need: the product pages (saved as PDFs or pasted in), third-party sources such as Reddit threads, reviews and articles, plus your topic, audience and keywords
Prompt
Your task is to create a helpful, accurate and well-researched gift guide using the source material I’ve provided. Article topic: [e.g., 5 Best Christmas Gift Ideas for Runners] Target audience: [Describe who will be reading the article] Primary keyword: [Insert keyword] Additional keywords: [Insert relevant keywords] I’ve uploaded: 1. Product pages containing descriptions, specifications, features and pricing. 2. Third-party sources, including Reddit discussions, customer feedback and independent articles about these products. Step 1: Analyze the sources Before writing, review all the uploaded material. For each product, identify: - Key features and specifications - What makes it a suitable gift - Who would benefit most from receiving it - Common positives and negatives mentioned by real users - Potential drawbacks or purchasing considerations - Claims supported by the product manufacturer versus independent sources Prioritize facts supported by the supplied evidence. Do not invent product features, prices, customer experiences or performance claims. Clearly flag missing information and conflicting claims. Step 2: Identify useful insights Look for patterns across the third-party sources. What questions do buyers frequently ask? What problems are people trying to solve? What features matter most to the intended recipient? Use these findings to develop genuinely useful recommendations rather than repeating manufacturer descriptions. Step 3: Create the article Write an engaging, SEO-friendly gift guide that includes: - An introduction that directly addresses the reader’s search intent. - A useful summary of the recommended products. - A dedicated section for each product with a descriptive heading. - An explanation of why each product makes a good gift. - A “Best for” recommendation explaining which type of person would appreciate it. - Important considerations or drawbacks, where supported by evidence. Use natural language. Avoid generic marketing phrases, exaggerated claims and unnecessary filler. Step 4: Apply editorial and SEO quality checks Follow these rules: 1. Accuracy: Every product-specific claim must be supported by the supplied sources. 2. Experience: Never claim to have personally used, tested or reviewed a product unless I’ve provided evidence of that experience. 3. Originality: Synthesize information and add useful comparisons rather than copying or lightly rewriting the sources. 4. Trust: Attribute relevant third-party findings and provide links to the original sources when available. 5. Search intent: Prioritize helping readers choose a suitable gift over keyword repetition. 6. Transparency: Distinguish manufacturer claims from independent user feedback. 7. Helpful content: Explain why each recommendation deserves its place on the list. Step 5: Final deliverables Provide: 1. An SEO title tag and meta description. 2. The complete article with descriptive H2 and H3 headings. 3. A comparison table summarizing the recommended products. 4. A list of sources used, with URLs where available. 5. A fact-checking checklist identifying claims requiring manual verification. 6. Three opportunities to improve the article with original information, expert input or first-hand experience. Important: Do not begin writing immediately. First, present your research findings in a table, highlight any evidence gaps, and propose an article outline. Wait for my approval before drafting the article.

How to use it

  1. Tap Copy prompt, paste it into your AI tool and fill in the four [placeholders] at the top.
  2. Upload the product pages and third-party sources.
  3. Review the research table and outline it sends back, then reply to approve the draft.
