Example session

What a session might look like

Illustration · Post-redesign traffic dropExample
The situation
A company relaunched its website. Six weeks later, organic traffic is down by a third. The agency says it's an algorithm update. The developer says the redirects are fine. Nobody agrees on what to do.
What we'd do in the session
  1. Compare Search Console data before and after launch to see which pages and queries lost visibility, rather than looking at sitewide totals.
  2. Check whether the losses cluster in one section, one template or one type of query.
  3. Test a sample of old URLs against the redirect map to see whether they land on the right new pages, or on the homepage, a category or a 404.
  4. Look at what changed on the pages that lost the most: content removed, internal links lost, titles rewritten, pages left out of the sitemap.
What you'd leave with
A list of the pages and queries that lost visibility, the most likely cause for each group, and a clear first task for the developer. Something like “fix these 40 redirects that point to the homepage and restore the internal links from the old category pages” instead of “look into the traffic drop.”
