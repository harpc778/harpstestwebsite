AI Visibility Audit Service Terms

These terms apply to purchases of the AI Visibility Audit and Audit + 90-Day Roadmap through harpsdigital.com. The services are supplied by 1442211 B.C. Ltd., doing business as Harps Digital, at 1185–329 Howe Street, Vancouver, B.C. V6C 3N2, Canada. Support email: harpreet@harpsdigital.com. These packages are offered for business use. If you buy on behalf of a business, you confirm you’re authorized to make the purchase.

What you’re buying

  • AI Visibility Audit · $1,950 USD. A review of 20 buyer prompts and two competitors across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. Includes technical SEO and AI crawlability, a review of priority pages, a brand fact-check, a written findings report with the 10 fixes to prioritize, and a 45-minute walkthrough.
  • Audit + 90-Day Roadmap · $2,950 USD. A review of 50 buyer prompts and five competitors across the same platforms, plus deeper topic, competitor, and citation-source analysis. Includes a written findings report, a 90-day roadmap prioritized by impact and effort, a plan for which pages should answer key buyer questions, and a 90-minute working session.

Each package is a one-time project. Each order covers the business website and primary market Harps Digital confirms in writing. Any additional website, market, or other change to the agreed scope requires written approval and may change the price and timing. Applicable taxes and the total payable appear before you pay. Implementation and ongoing monitoring can be agreed separately in writing. Findings reflect the answers and sources observed during the audit. AI answers can change, and the service does not guarantee future rankings, mentions, citations, traffic, or revenue.

How buyer prompts are selected

Harps Digital selects the buyer prompts based on the products or services, target audience, and primary market confirmed at kickoff. The prompts reflect how buyers might look for a solution, compare providers, or ask for a recommendation. You can suggest questions during intake. Harps Digital makes the final selection within your package scope and lists the prompts tested in your report. New prompts or re-testing requested after testing begins can be scoped separately.

Kickoff, timing, and delivery

Enter your business name and the full URL of the website you want audited at checkout. If the URL is wrong, email Harps Digital as soon as you notice. Harps Digital will correct a typo before work starts. If the correction involves a different website or changes the scope, Harps Digital will confirm any changes to the scope, fee, or timing with you in writing before proceeding.

Within 24 hours of successful payment, Harps Digital will email you to confirm your kickoff date. Kickoff is scheduled after the 24-hour cancellation window. Complete the intake and provide the information and agreed access needed for the audit before kickoff. Harps Digital will only ask for read-only access where access is needed and will never ask for your passwords. The expected report turnaround is 7–10 business days from the confirmed kickoff, once the required inputs are in place. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding B.C. statutory holidays. If required inputs are missing or delayed, Harps Digital may pause work and confirm a revised kickoff or delivery date by email.

Harps Digital sends the report to the email address on the order, or to another address you confirm in writing. Both packages include a call, scheduled separately at a mutually agreed time. The service is complete once the report and any included roadmap have been sent and the included call has taken place, or an alternative to the call has been agreed to in writing and provided. If the call has not been booked within 30 days of the report being sent, despite Harps Digital offering times, the service is considered complete.

If you don’t complete the intake within 60 days of payment, despite reminders from Harps Digital, the order may be closed and treated as a change-of-mind cancellation.

Revisions and corrections

Your package includes one round of consolidated feedback. Email your feedback within 10 business days after the walkthrough. Harps Digital will clarify the findings and correct factual errors or omissions within the agreed scope. Requests to test new prompts, add competitors or markets, or re-run the audit can be scoped and priced separately.

Cancellations and refunds

To cancel for a full refund, email your request to harpreet@harpsdigital.com within 24 hours of successful payment. The deadline is measured from the payment timestamp and includes weekends and holidays. Your request must be sent within the window. Harps Digital can process the refund after the window closes.

After 24 hours, the fee is non-refundable for change-of-mind cancellations, even if kickoff is scheduled for later, unless Harps Digital has agreed to different terms with you in writing. If Harps Digital cancels before work begins, you’ll receive a full refund. If Harps Digital cannot provide a material part of the agreed service after work starts, it will offer a reasonable way to complete the service or refund the portion of the fee for the work it cannot deliver. These terms do not limit any rights or remedies that cannot be waived under applicable law.
