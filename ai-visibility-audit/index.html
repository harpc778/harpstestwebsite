The shift

Your buyers are asking AI for a shortlist.

Buyers now ask ChatGPT and Claude who to hire and what to buy. If they Google instead, Google's AI Overviews hands them a shortlist anyway.

If you're not in the answer, you're not on the list.

The AI Visibility Audit answers three questions:

01

Do you show up?

When buyers ask for a recommendation in your category, does your business come up?

02

What does AI say about you?

Is it accurate, current and favourable, or is it repeating old complaints and outdated information?

03

Which sources shape the answer?

Which pages, directories, reviews and competitors is AI pulling from?

Myth vs reality

Five AI search myths.

Myth
AEO replaces SEO.
Reality

AI answers lean on pages that already rank and earn trust. Good SEO is good GEO.

Myth
An llms.txt file gets you into ChatGPT.
Reality

There's little evidence the major AI tools rely on it today. Crawl access and the content itself matter far more.

Myth
Schema markup alone gets you cited.
Reality

Schema helps AI understand who you are and what you offer. It doesn't earn citations on its own.

Myth
You can't measure AI visibility.
Reality

You can. Tracked buyer prompts, mention and citation share, and AI referral traffic all give you a baseline to measure against.

Myth
Being mentioned is enough.
Reality

What AI says about you matters as much as whether it mentions you.

Good SEO is good GEO. The audit checks both.

What's covered

What the audit covers.

01
Technical SEO and AI crawlability

Can AI crawlers reach, render and understand your key pages? I check bot access, rendering, indexation, site structure and schema.

02
Content and topical coverage

Do your pages answer the questions buyers ask before they shortlist? I map your content against the topics and comparisons that drive recommendations.

03
AI visibility across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and AI Overviews

I test real buyer prompts and record where you appear, where you don't, and which sources get cited.

04
Your brand in AI answers: fact or fiction?

What does AI actually say about your brand? I check your pricing, features, reputation and positioning in AI answers against the truth. Then I trace where outdated or wrong claims come from, whether that's an old review, a forum thread or a competitor's comparison page.

05
Where competitors win, and why

I show which competitors appear instead of you, and the pages and sources that put them there.

All of it ends in a prioritized findings report: what to fix, in what order, and what to measure.

The problem
For local injury prompts (“best car accident lawyer in [city]”), AI tools were recommending competing firms.
What we did
Tracked AI visibility across ChatGPT and Google's AI features, ran a content-gap analysis against the competitors winning those answers, and audited the location pages.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is this different from a regular SEO audit?
A regular SEO audit looks at rankings and site health. This audit covers that too, then tests what AI tools actually say when buyers ask about your category, who they recommend and which sources they trust.
Which AI tools do you test?
ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews.
Can you guarantee we'll be cited by AI?
No. Nobody can. AI answers change constantly and no one controls them. The audit reflects what AI tools showed during the audit period and the changes most likely to improve your visibility. It doesn't guarantee future rankings, mentions, citations, traffic or revenue.
How do you choose the prompts?
I choose them based on what you sell, who buys it and your primary market, which we confirm at kickoff. They reflect how buyers look for a solution, compare providers and ask for recommendations. You can suggest questions during intake, and every prompt tested is listed in your report.
What if AI is saying something wrong about us?
That's part of the audit. I flag inaccurate or outdated claims, find the sources they likely come from, and recommend what to publish or update to correct them.
Do you implement the fixes?
The audit is a one-time project. Your team can run with the findings, or if you'd like hands-on help afterwards, we can agree that separately.
How long does it take, and what do you need from us?
Your report arrives within 7–10 business days of your confirmed kickoff. Before kickoff, I'll need a short intake on what you sell and who buys it, your main competitors, and, if possible, read-only access to Google Search Console. I'll never ask for passwords.
What if something in the report is wrong?
Your package includes one round of feedback within 10 business days of the walkthrough. I'll clarify findings and correct any factual errors or omissions.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Email within 24 hours of payment for a full refund. After that, the fee is non-refundable for change-of-mind cancellations. See the service terms for details.
Can we re-run the audit later?
Yes. The report includes your baseline prompts, so we can re-test the same questions and measure progress.
